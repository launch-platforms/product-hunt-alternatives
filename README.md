# Product Hunt Alternatives

The best Product Hunt alternatives for launching a startup or SaaS product, compared by **Domain Rating (DR)**, **cost**, **link type** (dofollow or nofollow) and **who each platform suits**.

**Last verified: 2026-10-28.** DR comes from Ahrefs and is refreshed automatically every month. Link types come from live listings we checked; platforms change them, so inspect a current listing before you count on one.

> Full guide with a write-up of every platform: **[Best Product Hunt alternatives](https://discoverlaunches.com/alternatives/product-hunt)**

## Why look beyond Product Hunt?

Product Hunt (DR 91) is still the biggest launch day on the internet. It is also one day long, crowded with teams that arrive with a prepared audience, and its links to your site are **nofollow**, so they bring visitors but no ranking value. Most makers do best with one main launch and three to six smaller ones over the following two weeks.

## Which one to pick

- **A dofollow link from a strong domain:** Fazier, Uneed, SaaSHub, Tiny Startups, DevHunt.
- **A calmer, longer launch window:** Peerlist, Microlaunch, Open Launch, DiscoverLaunches.
- **Developer tools:** Hacker News (Show HN), DevHunt, Open Launch.
- **Signups before you launch:** BetaList.
- **Feedback from founders:** Indie Hackers, Hacker News.
- **B2B buyers after launch:** G2, once you have customers to ask for reviews.

## Terms used above

**Domain Rating (DR)** is Ahrefs' 0–100 estimate of how strong a site's backlink profile is. A link from a high-DR site carries more weight, and the scale gets harder to climb the higher you go.

**Dofollow vs nofollow.** A normal (dofollow) link passes some of the linking site's authority to yours, which helps your pages rank. A link marked `nofollow`, `ugc` or `sponsored` asks search engines not to pass it. Nofollow links still bring visitors; they just do little for your rankings. For a new domain, a few relevant dofollow links are one of the fastest ways to start ranking.
